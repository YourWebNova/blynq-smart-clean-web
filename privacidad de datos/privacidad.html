Privacy Policy

Your trust is our top priority. Learn how BLYNQ Cleaning Services LLC protects and manages your personal information.

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

Welcome to BLYNQ Cleaning Services LLC ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe online experience. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or contact us regarding our residential and commercial cleaning services in Minot, North Dakota, and surrounding areas.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, as well as automatic information generated when you navigate our website:

  • Personal Information You Provide: When you fill out a contact form, request a quote, or reach out to us via email or phone, we may collect personal identification details such as your full name, email address, phone number, and physical address or service location details.
  • Automated Information & Analytics: When you access our website, we may automatically collect non-personal browser data, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages viewed. We may utilize analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or similar third-party services) to help us analyze website traffic and improve user experience.
  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance functionality and collect statistics on visitor interaction. You can instruct your web browser to refuse all cookies or notify you when a cookie is being sent.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to your inquiries, quote requests, and customer service needs.
  • Scheduling and confirming cleaning service appointments.
  • Sending important administrative updates or follow-up communications related to your requested services.
  • Analyzing website performance and user behavior to optimize our site layout and services.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and protecting our business against fraud or unauthorized access.

3. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or lease your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may share necessary details with trusted third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf (such as web hosting, analytics, email delivery, or payment processing). These providers are obligated to keep your information confidential.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose personal information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental regulations, or if we believe in good faith that such action is necessary to protect our legal rights, property, or safety.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable physical, technical, and administrative security measures designed to protect your personal information against loss, unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. However, please note that no transmission over the Internet or electronic storage method is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (such as social media pages or third-party scheduling tools). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have provided to us. If you wish to update or remove your details from our database, please contact us using the information provided below.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
